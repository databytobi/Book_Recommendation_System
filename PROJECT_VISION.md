Why you're building it.

Traditional recommendation approaches, such as displaying bestselling books or manually curated collections, often fail to capture the unique preferences of individual readers. As a result, users may receive recommendations that are generic, repetitive, or unrelated to their evolving interests. I am building this system in other to addesser the problem of repetitive recommendations and generic recommendations. i want the system to be able to recommend books to users based on their preferences and interests. just like spotify does for music.

The problem it solves.

This project addresses this challenge through the design and implementation of a Hybrid AI-Powered Book Recommendation and Discovery Platform. Rather than relying on a single recommendation technique, the platform combines multiple recommendation strategies to generate more accurate, diverse, and personalized recommendations. The result is a scalable system capable of serving both new users with limited interaction history and returning users with established reading preferences.

Target users. The target users are book lovers and any who wants a platfrom that knows them.

Current architecture.

Recommendation Architecture

The recommendation engine consists of several independent modules working together.

The primary components include:

- Popularity-Based Recommendation
- Collaborative Filtering
- Content-Based Recommendation
- Semantic Recommendation
- Hybrid Recommendation Engine
- Recommendation Explanation Engine
- Recommendation Cache
